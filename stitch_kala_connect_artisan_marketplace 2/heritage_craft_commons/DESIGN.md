## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an AI-assisted marketplace bridging rural and regional Indian craftspeople directly with conscious domestic and global buyers. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Rooted & Authentic:** Evokes the tactile dignity of natural fibers, clay, loom weights, and hand-chiseled wood. The interface avoids cold silicon-valley minimalism in favor of a warm, archival, gallery-like intimacy.
- **Empathetic & Plainspoken:** Designed with extreme accessibility for diverse linguistic backgrounds and varying digital literacy. Affordances are generous, obvious, and supportive.
- **Fair & Reverent:** Highlighting craft provenance and regional lineage above aggregate consumer volume. The visual presence respects the master artisan as a studio creator rather than an anonymous supplier.

### Aesthetic Movement
**Heritage Tactile Minimalism:** A hybrid of calm, high-legibility digital utility and classical editorial printing. It pairs an organic, unbleached textile ground with precise hairline frames (#E4DFD3), quiet artisanal pigment accents, and stately literary serifs. The visual tone remains calm, uncluttered, and trustworthy.

## Colors

The palette balances warm organic earths, stone pigments, and deep indigo ink to reflect hand-dyed materials while maintaining rigorous contrast.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Surface:**
  - Base Viewport Background: Warm ivory `#F7F5EF` creates an earthy, paper-like warmth without screen glare.
  - Surface & Card Background: Crisp pure white `#FFFFFF` elevates product imagery and tactile photography.
  - Hairline Separators & Structural Borders: `#E4DFD3`, a warm bone tone for soft physical demarcation.
- **Core Tones:**
  - **Primary (`#79A8AE` - Dusty Teal):** Interactive highlights, key interactive buttons, active tab indicators, and search actions.
  - **Secondary (`#D1AD6D` - Muted Amber):** Secondary calls to action, high-value inquiries, and regional provenance highlights.
  - **Tertiary (`#9CAF9A` - Sage):** Trust markers, verified artisan endorsements, GI tag confirmations, and 'Artisan Approved' validation.
  - **Neutral (`#34354F` - Deep Ink/Navy):** Primary text and structural icon fills, offering high legibility and avoiding harsh digital pure blacks.
- **Contextual Categorical Accents:**
  - Dusty Rose (`#B58F91`) and Lavender (`#9A8EAC`) are reserved specifically for categorical taxonomy tags, textile weave classifications, and regional geography pills.

## Typography

The typography leverages a dual-family strategy:

1. **Noto Serif (Headlines & Editorial Focus):** Communicates cultural permanence, artisanal prestige, and historical gravitas. Used for craft titles, artisan biographies, category showcases, and section markers.
2. **Inter (Body, UI Controls, & Metadata):** A neutral, highly legible sans-serif optimized for functional clarity across mobile screen scales, multilingual interfaces, operational specs, and transaction flows.

### Principles
- Preserve high contrast between the Deep Ink (`#34354F`) text and the `#F7F5EF` / `#FFFFFF` surfaces.
- For regional scripts (such as Devanagari, Bengali, Tamil, etc.), pair Noto Serif with native Google Noto fonts at matching x-heights and line heights to maintain parity.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on a strict **8px baseline spatial rhythm** with 4px half-steps used exclusively for compact badge paddings and inline status markers.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Mobile (viewport &lt; 768px): 4 columns, 16px margins, 16px gutters.
  - Tablet (768px – 1024px): 8 columns, 24px margins, 20px gutters.
  - Desktop (&gt; 1024px): 12 columns, max-width 1280px container, centered, 24px gutters.
- **Top Utility & Search Zone:**
  - Header is search-forward and elevated as a persistent control bar. Voice search affordances (microphone icons) and vernacular language switchers maintain generous 48px minimum touch targets on mobile.
- **Horizontal Scrolling Strips:**
  - Category filters (Textiles, Pottery, Jewellery, Woodwork, Paintings, Regional Crafts) employ an edge-to-edge overflow on mobile with snap-alignment and 8px gaps between pill items.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid synthetic, heavy skeuomorphism, the elevation relies on **tonal shifts paired with crisp structural hairline outlines**.

- **Surface Layering:** 
  - Layer 0 (Canvas): Background `#F7F5EF`.
  - Layer 1 (Cards, Modules, Search Container): Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px solid `#E4DFD3` border.
  - Layer 2 (Floating Action Buttons, Search Menus, Bottom Modals): `#FFFFFF` elevated with a warm, low-opacity ambient drop shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(52, 53, 79, 0.08)`.
- **Ghost Borders:**
  - Elevation is primarily defined through separation lines rather than deep drop shadows. A 1px outline in `#E4DFD3` brings deliberate structure to product cards and badge surfaces.

## Shapes

The design system uses balanced curvature to harmonize natural organic handcrafts with modern interface patterns.

- **Standard Elements (Cards, Dialogue Modals):** Standardized to 12px (`0.75rem`), providing a softly framed canvas for product photography without looking overly bubbly.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Search Bar):** 8px (`0.5rem`) for deliberate input discipline.
- **Micro-Badges & Regional Chips:** 9999px (full capsule/pill) for craft taxonomies, origin indicators, and validation markers.
- **Image Containers:** Inherit the parent card radius (12px top-left, top-right) with clean edge-clipping.

## Components

### 1. Navigation & Search Header
- **Container:** White (`#FFFFFF`) with bottom hairline border (`1px solid #E4DFD3`).
- **Search Bar:** Deep input box with `#F7F5EF` interior, 1px `#E4DFD3` border, leading search icon, and a prominent voice search trigger with teal `#79A8AE` hover/active feedback.
- **Language Switcher:** Compact pill showing active script (e.g., "EN | हिन्दी") using `#34354F` text and an down-chevron indicator.

### 2. Category Strip
- **Form:** Horizontal scrolling row placed directly beneath search.
- **Items:** Text label paired with an optional micro-icon, rounded to 9999px. Unselected: `#FFFFFF` ground, 1px `#E4DFD3` border. Selected: `#79A8AE` background with pure white text.

### 3. Product & Artisan Cards
- **Structure:** 12px rounded white card framed in 1px `#E4DFD3`.
- **Imagery:** 4:3 or 1:1 craft photography with an inset top badge:
  - *Authentic Photo*: Semi-translucent dark pill (`rgba(52, 53, 79, 0.75)`) with white text and camera micro-icon.
- **Card Content:** 
  - Craft Origin Chip: Tiny capsule tag (e.g., "Rajasthan · Block print") styled in Dusty Rose (`#B58F91`) or Lavender (`#9A8EAC`) tint.
  - Title: Noto Serif `headline-sm`, Deep Ink (`#34354F`).
  - Validation: 'Artisan approved' checkmark row with Sage (`#9CAF9A`) icon and text.
  - Price & Action: Price in bold Inter `label-lg`, flanked by an inquiry button.

### 4. Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Purchase / Direct Message):** Solid Dusty Teal (`#79A8AE`), `#FFFFFF` text, 8px border-radius, 44px min-height.
- **Secondary Action (Enquire / Custom Order):** Warm Amber (`#D1AD6D`) text or outline, optional soft fill tint.
- **Voice / Mic Button:** Circular `#79A8AE` background with white icon, minimum 48px tap target.

### 5. Chips, Status Tags, and Badges
- **Artisan Approved Badge:** Light sage tint background (`#EEF3EE`), `#3E5C3E` deep forest text, solid `#9CAF9A` checkmark icon.
- **Craft Origin / Technique Chips:** Soft Dusty Rose (`#F7EFEF` bg, `#6F4B4D` text) and Lavender (`#F3F1F6` bg, `#4F475B` text).
- **GI (Geographical Indication) Tag:** Muted Amber (`#FDF8EE` bg, `#83652E` text, 1px border `#D1AD6D`).

### 6. Form Inputs & Selection
- **Input Fields:** 48px height, `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px `#E4DFD3` border, 8px corner radius. Focused state shifts border to `#79A8AE` with a 1px ring.
- **Radio & Checkboxes:** 20px controls with `#79A8AE` active state, paired with clear native-language labels.
