5 Rules for Best Results

  • 1. Slow 360° turn: Orbit slowly around your craft or rotate it smoothly on a table.
  • 2. Natural daylight: Shoot near a window or in a courtyard. Avoid direct camera flash.
  • 3. Close-ups: Bring camera close to show glaze crackle, weave density, or brushstrokes.
  • 4. Hands for scale: Hold or touch the object to show real physical scale.
  • 5. Privacy: Keep personal bills and documents outside the camera view.
